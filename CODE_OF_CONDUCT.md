# Code of conduct

Duckbook follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, insults, and sharing other people's private information are not accepted anywhere in this project's spaces.

Report problems privately to ndkhoa1000@gmail.com. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, and block people who don't follow this code, as described in the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
