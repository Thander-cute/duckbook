A personal planner

A planner that feels like paper

Month, week and day pages in pale-blue ink, after The Present Day Planner. Plan the day, look back on the week, and keep it all on your own computer.

  • Runs on your computer
  • No account
  • Optional sync to your own Google Drive

Five kinds of pages

  • MonthA theme, goals and a calendar with room to note each day.
  • WeekTasks of the week, an urgent/important grid and a habit tracker.
  • DayTasks with pomodoro estimates, gratitude, your day in one sentence and up to three photos.
  • ReflectLook back on the week in seven sentences, and on the month.
  • FocusPlace ideas on an impact and difficulty map, then plan them into weeks.

Your pages stay on your computer

Duckbook is an app you run yourself: a Windows desktop app, or the same notebook in a browser, served by a small program that only listens on your own computer. There is no Duckbook account and no Duckbook server on the internet.

Pages, photos and automatic backups are saved in a folder on your device. This website only describes the app: you cannot sign in here, and it holds no one’s notebook.

Optional sync through your Google Drive

To use the same notebook on more than one computer, connect your Google account in Settings → Data → Sync with Google Drive. Sync is off until you do.

What Duckbook does with Google

  • One narrow permission

    Duckbook asks only for drive.file: it can see and change the files it created, not the rest of your Drive. It does not ask for your name or email.

  • Straight to your Drive

    Pages and day photos go into a visible Duckbook Sync folder. Each computer talks to Google directly; the developer never receives them.

  • Not encrypted yet

    Synced files are readable by anyone who can open that Google account. An encrypted option is planned but not available yet.

  • Easy to stop

    Stop syncing → Stop and sign out asks Google to revoke access and removes the sign-in from your computer. If you are offline, use Google Account → Third-party connections.
